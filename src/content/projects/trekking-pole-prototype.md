An ongoing hardware R&D project: designing and testing a trekking pole built
around specific complaints I've had with off-the-shelf poles on technical
alpine approaches — grip placement, collapsed length for glacier travel, and
basket design for mixed snow/scree terrain.

## Approach

Rather than starting from a CAD model, this project started from the failure
modes of gear I already own — where existing poles felt wrong on a real
approach, and why. Early iterations are being tested directly in the field
(the only environment that actually tells you if a design decision was
right) and revised based on what breaks or feels wrong.

## Status

Personal R&D, in development — current iteration is being validated against
real alpine use before moving to a more refined prototype.
