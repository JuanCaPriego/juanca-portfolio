Pico de Orizaba (Citlaltépetl) tops out at 5,636 m, making it the highest point in
Mexico and the third-highest in North America. The standard Jamapa Glacier route is
straightforward technically, but the altitude and glacier travel demand real
preparation.

## Logistics

We based out of the Piedra Grande hut (~4,260 m) and moved to the glacier in the dark,
aiming to summit and be off the ice before the sun softened the snow.

## The climb

The lower slopes are loose scree — slow, grinding progress. Once on the glacier, crampons
and consistent pace matter more than raw fitness. The crater rim at sunrise, looking down
into the ice-lined caldera, was worth every step of the approach.

## Takeaway

This one reinforced something I keep relearning: altitude doesn't care how fit you are
at sea level. Acclimatization days are not optional.
