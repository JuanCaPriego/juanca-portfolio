After going into some german contryside towns and looking the landscapes, some farms and mountains, I got inspired on capturing a little of how those places made me feel and capture also the behaviour of nature in some sense. A friend of mine who studies photography was selling an old camera and I found a great opportunity to start.

## How I started
My sister took a few classes about photography and artistic vision at college which led her to learn how to use a camera, photo editing and photo compostion. I asked for tips and tutoring and started experimenting by going out on trips and taking the camera, taking photos of what I thought looked good and then doing some basic edits.

## Whhat challenges I have found on this
I think the greates challenge I have faced, more than a technical one has been mental. One thing I have felt whenever I see the pictures I take is that they aren't as good as I wished or sometimes don't capture the places I am looking at, but one thing that helped a lot was having friends to give me feedback on their artistic perspective which is based in a lot more experience.

Also, taking inspiration from multiple creators helps with looking with different lenses at many takes or angles. 

I am really looking fordward to keep making progress and have another motivation to keep finding new places.
