After a few seasons of mountaineering, I started looking for something to train with
between expeditions — something that didn't need a full weekend and a drive to the
mountains. Cross-country and trail running turned out to be the answer.

## What carries over from climbing

Pacing is pacing. The instinct to hold something back for the last third of a climb
maps almost directly onto negative-splitting a long run. Reading terrain — where to
save effort, where you can move fast — is the same skill in a different setting.

## What's different

Running has much faster feedback loops. A bad pacing decision on a mountain costs you
hours; on a training run it costs you a few uncomfortable minutes and a lesson for next
time. That's made it a good complement — lower stakes, faster iteration.

Currently working up to longer distances on technical trail terrain, with an eye toward
eventually tying this back into faster approaches for alpine routes.
