Iztaccíhuatl (5,230 m) — "the sleeping woman" — is one of the classic acclimatization
climbs before attempting Pico de Orizaba. The route crosses several sub-summits before
the final glacier approach, which makes it a longer day than its elevation alone suggests.

## The approach

We started from La Joya at around 4,000 m and moved through the night to catch a clear
sunrise above the cloud layer. Cold, still air, and the first light hitting the glacier
made the early alpine start worth it.

## What I'd do differently

- Start earlier — the glacier softens fast once the sun is up.
- Bring an extra layer for the ridge traverse; it's more exposed to wind than the lower
  approach suggests.

Outside of the technical notes, this climb is really about pacing — matching effort to
elevation and being honest with yourself about turnaround time.
